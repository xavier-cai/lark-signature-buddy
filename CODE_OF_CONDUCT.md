# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and keep technical
disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this
policy.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
